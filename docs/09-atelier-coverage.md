# Atelier coverage map

Where each journey, screen and staff surface from `docs/journeys/` lives in the code, and what is deliberately
not built. `docs/08-atelier-build-contract.md` is the contract this was built to; this document is the index
back from the specification to the implementation. Paths are repository-relative. Client routes are in the
`(atelier)` route group (no sign-in, no onboarding gate); staff routes are under `/admin/atelier` inside the
signed-in shell and call `requireAdminPage()` plus a role check in every function.

Conventions that hold everywhere:

- Every status word a client or staff member reads comes from a label map in `convex/shared/atelier/enums.ts`
  or from the server's own view (`statusLabel`, `stateLabel`, `kindLabel`), never from a string in a component.
- Every owned read goes through `useOwnerArgs` (`src/hooks/atelier/use-owner-args.ts`) and every owned
  mutation passes the guest key; the server resolves the actor (`convex/atelier/access.ts`).
- The stage figure is drawn from the spec by `src/components/atelier/studio/suit-figure.tsx`; `docs/10-atelier-studio-figure.md` lists what each choice changes.
- Every specification listing is an `OrderBook` with production ids (`src/components/atelier/common/order-book.tsx`);
  every status is a `StatusLine`.
- Nothing here takes a payment, sends a notification or books a slot. Payments are recorded by staff from the
  house's payment system; notifications are queued as records; a fitting is requested until the house confirms it.

## Cross-cutting acceptance

Contract §7 lists each acceptance scenario and where it is enforced. The short version:

- **Idempotency** — `submissionKey` on every request mutation (`cases.submit`, `appointments.request`,
  `corrections.request`, `privacy.request`); payment and refund records are idempotent on the provider's reference.
- **Truthful states** — the state-language table in contract §6; label maps in `enums.ts`; `StatusLine` everywhere.
- **Ownership** — `requireOwned` before any read by id; staff reads need a role; the organiser of a group never
  receives another participant's measurements, photographs or draft.
- **Retention** — hourly crons in `convex/crons.ts`: `previews.expireJobs`, `photos.expire`, `cases.escalateOverdue`,
  `quotes.expire`.
- **Audit** — every staff mutation writes an `atelierAuditLog` row through `audit()` in `convex/atelier/access.ts`.

Tests: `tests/atelier-*.test.mjs` cover the spec engine, the models (cases, appointments, quotes, orders,
corrections, privacy, groups, staff, fit, previews, catalogue, drafts, shares) and the fixtures;
`pnpm test` runs them with the rest of the suite.

## Deliberately not built, and known gaps

- **Payments and notifications.** No checkout and no sending. Staff record what the payment system reported;
  notifications are queued as records and the staff desk shows them as "Queued · not yet sent" until a provider
  is wired in.
- **Bookings.** No calendar integration: a fitting is requested, and staff confirm a time and booking reference
  from the house's own system.
- **Organiser conveniences in groups.** Editing the brief after creation and a participant changing consent after joining exist on the backend (`groups.update`, `groups.setConsent`) but have no control on the client pages yet; the organiser also cannot re-copy an invitation link, which is shown once.
- **S14 "Use this design"** from a previous preview is shown as "not available yet": the preview job view
  carries the spec hash and label but not the frozen spec; exposing `spec` on the job view or a
  `drafts.listRevisions` query would complete it.
- **Personal previews** need `OPENAI_API_KEY` on the deployment; without it the studio keeps the illustration
  and says so (`PREVIEWS_UNAVAILABLE`).
- **Cloth shades** are representative until staff confirm them on the catalogue desk.
- **Light theme** was captured on fewer screens than dark during the build; both use the same tokens.
- Smaller follow-ups (shared view fields such as `OrderView.draftId`, a finance-level eligibility read on the
  case, a concierge-level staff list for assignment, pagination on long lists) are listed in the contract's
  follow-up notes and are not blockers for the journeys above.
