## 2. The house

**Story.** Nyonisela Sioh was born in Monrovia, Liberia, displaced by the First Liberian Civil War, spent formative years in Europe and resettled in the United States. He launched Nyoni Couture in Charlotte in 2016 and expanded to an invitation-only Buckhead showroom in Atlanta (2024) and a Houston showroom (2025). Press describes the house as "blending African heritage with British, Italian and French tailoring".

**Positioning (site copy).** "An international seller and tailor of high-end bespoke fashion, offering made-to-measure custom suits and accessories for the discriminating cosmopolitan gentleman." Elsewhere: "a luxury brand for the discerning fashion connoisseur."

**Style descriptors (site copy).** "Natural, artistic, sophisticated, and romantic — heavily influenced by a combination of British, Italian, and French tailoring." Inspiration: "his African heritage, fused with Western styles."

**The Nyoni Couture Standard (site copy).** Built on three words: **Quality, Discretion, Craftsmanship.** Supporting claims: "fabrics handpicked from the world's finest mills", "one-to-one service in a refined setting", "precision fit personalized for the wearer", "style advice from experienced tailors", "craftsmanship delivered to your door".

**Clientele signals.** Celebrity clients named in press: Anthony Hamilton, Wyclef Jean, Tony McRae. A footwear line carries NBA player Bismack Biyombo's name ("Bismack Biyombo Luxury Shoes", made in Italy). Weddings, business and black-tie are the three occasions the site sells against.

## 3. Voice and tone

The site's own sentences set the register. Reuse these lines where they fit; they are the house's words, not ours.

**Copy deck (VERIFIED site copy).**

- "A bespoke suit is built around your exact measurements, posture and taste in premium cloth, with expert guidance at every step. It fits, feels and lasts in a way off-the-rack simply can't — an investment in how you show up."
- "Your suit is made to order and is typically ready in about six weeks from your first fitting."
- "Your process includes a private consultation with full measurements and a final fitting to perfect the result before delivery — with additional fittings if your garment needs them."
- "Adjustments are part of the service. If anything needs refining after your final fitting, we will make it right until the agreed fit is achieved."
- "Book a private fitting for a suit crafted around your measurements, style, and occasion — for weddings, business, and black tie events."
- "Two-piece: versatile tailoring for work and beyond." "Three-piece: complete, considered elegance." "Tuxedos: timeless formalwear for occasions."
- "Your suits are made to your measurements and scheduled across the year … so your wardrobe stays current."
- "Your measurements and preferences saved securely as part of your membership profile."
- "Membership is personal to you and is not transferable."
- "Circle Elite is invitation only — speak with your concierge to be considered."

**Tone rules derived from the copy.**

- Confident, plain, second person. Short declaratives. No exclamation marks.
- The house speaks as "we" and addresses "you"; a member has "your concierge".
- Vocabulary to use: cloth, commission, fitting, consultation, showroom, concierge, house, member, allowance, alteration, tailored fit, made-to-measure, bespoke.
- Vocabulary to avoid in member-facing copy: credits, tokens, render, upload, AI, generate, plan, subscription, tier (say "membership"), user.
- Luxury cues are behavioural, not typographic: "made right until the agreed fit is achieved" says more than any superlative.
- Spelling: the site is US-based; use US spelling in the app. (The Fitcheck stylist persona currently writes British English; change it.)

## 4. What the house sells, and how

### Bespoke and made-to-measure (VERIFIED)

- "Dozens of measurements taken by hand" at a private consultation; garment ready in about six weeks from the first fitting; final fitting before delivery; further fittings as needed; alterations included until the agreed fit is achieved.
- Rush timelines "can sometimes be arranged for weddings and special events"; a "Rush Fee 'Suit'" product exists in the store.
- Bespoke design is final once the order is placed and paid; custom and bespoke items are final sale.
- Cloth library sold as products (price band $1,795–$2,985 per commission): Loro Piana (S 170's) 713, Ermenegildo Zegna Trofeo 600 (summer cloth) 207, Lanificio di Crilu, "Myikgo" wool super 110's–140's (numbered 1, 3…), and the house's own "Kapra Sohini Collection".

### Ready-to-wear shop (VERIFIED)

- Returns within 30 days of delivery; unworn, unwashed, tags on, original packaging; altered garments are not accepted for credit.
- Shipping: standard 5–7 business days, express 2–3 business days.
- "Reserve in store": reserve online, try on at a showroom.
- "Virtual Appointment": video consultation "on your preferred video chat platform", with "personalized wardrobe advice and product recommendations".
- WishSuite wishlist buttons on every product.
- Size guide (jacket size = chest in inches; trouser waist ≈ jacket size − 6). Measurements it teaches: chest, sleeve (shoulder seam over a slightly bent arm to the wrist bone), jacket length (base of collar to mid-hand), natural waist, inseam (crotch seam to top of shoe).

"A made-to-measure suit each year, cut in Nyoni Fabric — with priority fittings, concierge support and access to experiences beyond bespoke." Three annual tiers, bought as WooCommerce products (checkout ids 19172, 19173, 19174); the page also says "Apply for Membership" with a review-and-approval step.

Benefits listed for members (higher tiers get the fuller set):

- First access to fittings and appointments (priority booking)
- Exclusive member savings on made-to-measure and bespoke (garments billed separately with savings applied)
- Complimentary alterations on all qualifying orders
- Personal concierge for styling, fittings and special requests
- First to discover new collections and limited releases ("first look at new arrivals")

Unknowns to confirm with the client: tier prices, the member-savings percentage, what "qualifying orders" means for free alterations, whether unused suit allowances roll over, and whether the allowance covers a two-piece only or any garment of equivalent value.

## 6. Visual identity (VERIFIED by capture of the live site, 21 Sep 2026)

The site is dark: a near-black ground with cream type and gold as the single accent, serif display headlines with gold italic emphasis ("Tailored to Fit. Designed to _Impress_."), gold buttons with black text, square corners on inputs. - **Imagery:** black-and-white and low-key colour photography of the tailor at work, suits on dark backgrounds, a catalogue grid of garments on black, client portraits. Product photography on the store is studio-lit on plain backgrounds.

## 7. Service gaps the app can close

Third-party reviews praise fit and craftsmanship and criticise **communication and delays around appointments and fittings**. The site also sells a membership whose promises ("priority booking", "fittings at your convenience", "scheduled across the year", "saved measurements") are exactly the things a members app can make visible and reliable:

1. A live commission tracker (consultation → measured → cloth chosen → cutting → first fitting → final fitting → ready) with dates.
2. A fittings calendar with reminders, rescheduling and showroom details.
3. A measurements profile the member can see, with history and who took them.
4. A concierge channel with a response-time promise, backed by staff tooling.
5. The allowance clock: "your next Signature suit unlocks on 1 March; book your consultation now."
