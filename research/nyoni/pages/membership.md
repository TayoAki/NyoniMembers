## Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Nyoni Circle membership include?

Every tier includes a made-to-measure suit each year, cut in Nyoni Fabric — the grade of cloth rises with your tier, from signature Nyoni Fabric at Signature, to premium at Prestige, to our finest cloths at Circle Elite. Every tier also brings priority booking, complimentary alterations and concierge support, with saved style profiles, early access and private-event invitations at the higher tiers.

How does billing work?

Membership is billed annually — US$549 for Signature, US$749 for Prestige, US$949 for Circle Elite. Your suit is made to your measurements and scheduled with your fitting, cut in the Nyoni Fabric grade of your tier. Membership renews each year unless you tell us otherwise.

Who is the Nyoni Circle membership for?

It is designed for the modern gentleman who values his time, his wardrobe and a relationship with a house that knows his measurements, his taste and his calendar.

Are fittings included in the membership?

Members receive priority access to fittings and appointments. Prestige and Circle Elite members enjoy fittings at their convenience; garments and made-to-measure work are billed separately with your member savings applied.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my membership?

Yes. You may move between tiers at any time, with the difference prorated — your next suit is simply cut in the Nyoni Fabric grade of your new tier.

Is the membership transferable?

Membership is personal to you and is not transferable. Gift memberships for a family member or colleague can be arranged through our concierge.
