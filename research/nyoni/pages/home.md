## Why made-to-measure _fits better_

Off-the-rack is cut for an average body — then tailored to hide the gaps. Yours isn’t average. We start from your measurements, so the fit is built in, not corrected after.

![](https://nyonicouture.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/08-measuring-tape.png)

### Measured to you

Dozens of measurements taken by hand — not a size label that guesses at your body.

![](https://nyonicouture.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/05-tailors-mannequin.png)

### Drafted to your frame

A pattern built for your posture, shoulders and stance, so the line stays clean when you move.

![](https://nyonicouture.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/01-needle-thread.png)

### Hand-finished to last

A canvassed construction that shapes to you over time, refined across three fittings.

## Everything You Need to Know  Before You _Book._

FAQs

01Is bespoke worth the investment?

A bespoke suit is built around your exact measurements, posture and taste in premium cloth, with expert guidance at every step. It fits, feels and lasts in a way off-the-rack simply can't — an investment in how you show up.

02How long does a suit take to make?

Your suit is made to order and is typically ready in about six weeks from your first fitting. Rush timelines can sometimes be arranged for weddings and special events.

03How many fittings are included?

Your process includes a private consultation with full measurements and a final fitting to perfect the result before delivery — with additional fittings if your garment needs them.

04What if adjustments are needed?

Adjustments are part of the service. If anything needs refining after your final fitting, we'll make it right until the agreed fit is achieved.

05Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. We dress clients across the country and internationally, with worldwide delivery straight to your door.
